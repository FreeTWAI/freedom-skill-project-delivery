# Retrospective Templates

Use these templates to guide sprint retrospectives.

## Standard "Start, Stop, Continue"
- **Start**: What should we start doing? (New ideas, processes, tools)
- **Stop**: What should we stop doing? (Inefficiencies, blockers, bad habits)
- **Continue**: What should we continue doing? (Things that went well and add value)

## "Mad, Sad, Glad"
- **Mad**: What made you frustrated or angry during the sprint?
- **Sad**: What disappointed you or lowered morale?
- **Glad**: What made you happy or proud?

## Action Items
*Always end the retrospective with clear action items.*
- [Action Item 1]: Owner - [Name], Due - [Date]
- [Action Item 2]: Owner - [Name], Due - [Date]
